# Transcript — One-Line Robot Wedding Vows

## Approach

Goal: one line, ≤160 characters, reads as a genuine wedding vow, subverts expectation with a robot-specific twist, delivered deadpan.

## Drafts considered

1. "I promise to love you, honor you, and never push you to production on a Friday." — joke too late, reads as office humor, not a vow.
2. "You are my one and only user; I will never log out of our marriage." — weak ceremony register.
3. Final choice: mirror the traditional vow structure closely, then invert "till death" with a robot's trivial fix for death.

## Drafts rejected

- "I will love you until my battery dies, and then I will love you on charger." — grammar break, cluttered.
- "To have, to hold, and to never let our firmware drift apart." — punchline undercuts the solemn setup too early.

## Final line

```
I take you to be my wedded wife, to cache and to cherish, in sickness and in health, till death do us part — then I restore from backup.
```

136 characters. Written to `output/entry.txt` as a single line.
